# fitting

A dogma engine. `fitting.Compute(fit.FittingInput())` works out a fit's resists, EHP, repair, sensors, and ewar resistance from `dogma.json`, with all skills V and every module active but an Assault Damage Control, left online so its brief burst isn't counted (`brieflyActiveEffects`); `gui.fitStats` runs it for each lost fit during the search. It reads the SDE's modifiers generically, and codes by hand only what they can't express (`effects.go`, `rah.go`, `stats.go`). `go test ./fitting` checks it against Pyfa's numbers for the fits in `fitting/testdata/golden` (regenerated by the harness there). See `.claude/docs/fits-plan.md` Phase 4.
